--- title: Privacy policy description: What information Physics Decoded collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. date: 2026-11-02 ---

This policy explains what happens to information when you use Physics Decoded (“we”, “the site”). In short: you don't need an account, your study progress stays on your device, and we collect as little as possible.

Information stored on your device

The site uses your browser's local storage (not cookies) to remember your quiz answers and scores, the pages you opened recently (for “Continue where you left off”), your light/dark theme choice and a running timed test. This information never leaves your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

Pages you open are also saved by the browser (a “service worker”) so they work offline. This copy is also only on your device.

Analytics

We count page views with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which does not use cookies and does not track you across websites. We also count anonymous study events, such as “a Grade XII chapter 4 MCQ set was finished” or a search term, to learn which topics students need. These counts are not linked to you and contain no name, email or device identifier.

Advertising

To keep the site free we may show ads from Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites, and to measure ads. You can turn off personalised ads in Google Ad Settings, and learn more in how Google uses information from sites that use its services. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. Ads are labelled, and we ask Google to block categories that are not appropriate for students.

Messages and reports you send

When you use the contact form or “Report a mistake”, we receive what you type, the page it is about, and the time. If you include your email address we use it only to reply. Messages are kept for up to 12 months and are not shared with anyone. To protect the forms from spam, a shortened, one-way scrambled form of your network address is used to limit how many messages can be sent at once; it is deleted within a day.

Videos

Video lessons are embedded from YouTube in privacy-enhanced mode. Nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press play.

Children

The site is made for students of Grade XI and XII. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and nothing on the site requires you to share any.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date above changes too. Questions about privacy: use the contact page.
