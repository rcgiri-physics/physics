--- title: Editorial policy description: How Physics Decoded writes, checks and corrects its notes, questions and model papers. date: 2026-11-02 ---

Written for the NEB syllabus

Every page is tied to a chapter of the NEB Grade XI or XII physics curriculum (2076). Notes cover the topics the syllabus lists for that chapter, and model papers follow the NEB specification grid (Group A multiple choice, Group B short answers, Group C long answers).

How content is made

  • Notes, questions and solutions are written and reviewed by the author.
  • AI tools are sometimes used to help draft questions or explanations. Nothing is published without a person checking the physics, the numbers and the answer key first.
  • Before anything is published, an automatic check rejects broken files: missing answers, duplicate options, wrong chapter numbers and similar problems.

Corrections

Every lesson has a Report a mistake button. Reports go straight to the author, and confirmed mistakes are corrected, usually within a few days. The “Updated” date on each page shows when it last changed.

Independence

Ads never influence what is written. Ads are labelled and kept apart from the content, and no ad is shown inside a question or during a timed test.
