What this site is

Physics Decoded is a free study site for Grade XI and XII physics in Nepal, following the NEB curriculum (2076) chapter by chapter. Everything is written for the board exam: clear notes with worked examples, multiple-choice practice with an explanation for every answer, full model question papers with solutions, and lab practicals written the way a lab copy needs them.

Who writes it

The notes and questions are written by Richesh Sharma, who also explains each chapter on the @rsaphysics YouTube channel. Content is checked against the NEB syllabus and corrected whenever a student reports a mistake. Read how content is made and checked in our editorial policy.

What you will find

  • Grade XI and Grade XII: every chapter of the syllabus, with its topics, notes and MCQ sets.
  • Model Questions: full NEB-pattern papers with a timed exam mode and worked solutions.
  • Lab practicals: aim, theory, procedure, observation tables, results and viva questions.
  • Search: find any topic, filtered by grade, type and topic area.

How it works for students

  • Free and open: no account, no sign-up and no paywall.
  • Your progress stays on your device: quiz answers and scores are saved in your browser, not on our servers.
  • Works on slow connections and offline: pages are small, and the pages you open are saved for offline reading.
  • Student-friendly ads: a few clearly labelled ads keep the site free. They never appear inside a question, and they are hidden during a timed test.
