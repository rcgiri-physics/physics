Group A — Multiple choice (11 × 1 = 11)

Choose the best alternative.

  1. The dimensional formula of moment of inertia is [1]
    1. \([ML^2T^0]\)
    2. \([MLT^{-2}]\)
    3. \([ML^2T^{-2}]\)
    4. \([M^0L^2T^0]\)
    Answer

    (a) \([ML^2T^0]\), since \(I = \sum m r^2\).

Group B — Short answer questions (8 × 5 = 40)

  1. (a) State the principle of conservation of angular momentum. [1]

    (b) A disc of moment of inertia \(0.2\ \text{kg m}^2\) rotates at \(10\ \text{rad s}^{-1}\). Find its kinetic energy. [2]

    Answer

    (b) \(K = \tfrac12 I\omega^2 = \tfrac12 (0.2)(10)^2 = 10\ \text{J}\).

Group C — Long answer questions (3 × 8 = 24)

  1. Derive an expression for the impedance of a series LCR circuit and obtain the condition for resonance. [5]

    Answer outline

    \(Z = \sqrt{R^2 + (X_L - X_C)^2}\); resonance when \(X_L = X_C\), i.e. \(f_0 = \dfrac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC}}\).
